Your Impact

Where Your Money Goes

We're a lean, volunteer-run program. Each session costs about $300 — every dollar goes directly to students.

📚

Books

Every student receives their own copy of our current read to keep. We choose titles featuring diverse characters, brave protagonists, and stories that open new worlds.

🔬

STEM & Craft Supplies

Coding kits, art supplies, science materials, seeds for herb gardens — the hands-on project materials that make every session come alive and connect books to the real world.

📦

Mailed Packages

We ship everything directly to each student before every session — books, supplies, and materials. The postage alone is part of what makes SPARKS magical. Opening the box is half the fun.
