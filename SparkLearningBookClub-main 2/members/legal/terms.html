The program

SPARKS Book Club is a free virtual book club for 3rd–5th grade students. Each month during the school year we mail enrolled students a book and activity kit and meet online to discuss it.

Accounts

A parent or guardian creates and owns the family account and is responsible for the student login they create. Leader and director accounts are granted by SPARKS directors.

Acceptable use

Be kind. Do not share login details, attempt to access other families' information, or use the portal for anything other than participating in SPARKS.

Materials

Books and kits are gifts from SPARKS to the student and are funded by donors. Please keep your mailing address up to date so nothing goes missing.
