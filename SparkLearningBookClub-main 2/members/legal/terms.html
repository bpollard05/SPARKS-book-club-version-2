1. What SPARKS is

SPARKS Book Club, Inc. is a nonprofit that runs a free virtual book club for elementary school students. Each month during the school year, enrolled students receive a book and an activity kit by mail and meet online with a volunteer leader to talk about the story and do a hands-on project. Participation is free. Books and kits are gifts to the student, paid for by donors.

2. Accounts

  • Family accounts are created and owned by a parent or legal guardian who is at least 18. You are responsible for keeping your password private and for everything done with your account.
  • Student logins are created by the parent from inside the family account. The parent is responsible for the student's use of the portal and may change or remove the login at any time.
  • Leader and director accounts are granted by SPARKS directors to approved volunteers and officers. Volunteers agree to use student information only to run their club and never to contact a student outside the program.
  • One person may hold more than one role (for example, a leader who is also a parent).

3. Using the portal

You agree to:

  • give accurate information and keep your mailing address current, since a wrong address means your student gets no book;
  • use the portal only to take part in SPARKS;
  • not share your login, try to access other families' information, or interfere with the portal.

Students may use the portal only to see their book and schedule and to send feedback to their leader. The portal has no chat, and we ask that students not attempt to use it for anything else.

4. Sessions and materials

  • Sessions are held online. Session dates and links are shown in the portal and may change; the calendar feed and the portal always show the current schedule.
  • We mail one book and one activity kit per enrolled student before each session, while supplies and funding allow. Packages are shipped to the address in the family account at the time the order is placed.
  • If a package cannot be delivered because of an incorrect address, we will contact you and try again once where possible.
  • Activity kits may contain small parts and ordinary craft or science supplies. An adult should supervise younger students during activities.

5. Online safety

Sessions are led by SPARKS volunteers who have agreed to our conduct expectations. Parents are responsible for supervising their student's online participation, including during video sessions. Please report any concern about a session or a volunteer to a director right away at becca@sparkslearning.org.
