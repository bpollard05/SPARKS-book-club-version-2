Program Participation Consent

SPARKS Book Club, Inc. · Version 2026-10 · Effective when published on sparkslearning.org · Questions: becca@pollard.us

Please read this consent before enrolling your student. You accept it when you create your family account, and you can review it any time on the Consents tab.

Who is agreeing

I am the parent or legal guardian of the student(s) listed in my SPARKS family account. I am at least 18 years old and have the authority to enroll them.

What I am consenting to

  • Participation. My student may take part in SPARKS Book Club, a free virtual book club with monthly online sessions led by SPARKS volunteers.
  • Mailed materials. SPARKS may mail a book and an activity kit to the address in my family account before each session. I will keep that address current and understand that materials are shipped to the address on file when the order is placed.
  • Online sessions. My student may join sessions by video link. I will supervise my student's participation as I see fit. I understand that sessions are group settings with other students and SPARKS volunteers, and that SPARKS volunteers are not responsible for supervising my student outside of session time.
  • Student login. I will create and control my student's login to the portal. I understand the portal has no chat and that the only thing my student can send is structured feedback to their leader, which I can always see.
  • Information. SPARKS may collect and use my family's information as described in the Privacy Policy, which I have read. For my student this means first name, grade, and club only.
  • Activities. Activity kits may include ordinary craft and science supplies and small parts. I will provide any adult supervision my student needs for hands-on activities.

What I am not consenting to

  • SPARKS will not share my student's information with anyone other than the service providers named in the Privacy Policy, and never for advertising.
  • SPARKS will not record video sessions or publish photos, names, or work of my student without a separate written permission from me.
  • SPARKS volunteers will not contact my student directly outside of program sessions.

My choices

I may withdraw my student from the program at any time from my family account, or by emailing becca@pollard.us. Withdrawal stops future packages immediately; a package already shipped will still arrive. I may also ask SPARKS to delete my family's information.

Emergency contact

I will provide an emergency contact in my family account. SPARKS will use it only if a concern about my student's safety or wellbeing arises during a session and I cannot be reached.

By accepting this consent at signup, I confirm that I have read it and agree to it on behalf of my student(s).
