Program Participation Consent

Version 2026-10-draft · SPARKS Book Club, Inc.

DRAFT PLACEHOLDER. SPARKS will supply the final Program Participation Consent text (PRD §3.1). Replace this page's content and publish a new version from the Director dashboard → Settings → Agreements so families are asked to re-accept it.

I am the parent or legal guardian of the student(s) listed in my family account. I give permission for my student to participate in SPARKS Book Club, including:

  • receiving books and activity materials by mail at the address in my account;
  • joining monthly virtual sessions led by SPARKS volunteer leaders;
  • using a student login that I create and control, to see their book, schedule, and send feedback to their leader.

I understand sessions are online, that SPARKS volunteers are not responsible for supervising my child outside the session, and that I can withdraw my student at any time from my Family account.
