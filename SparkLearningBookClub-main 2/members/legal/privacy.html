What we collect

From parents/guardians: name, email, phone, mailing address (used only to ship books and activity kits), and an emergency contact.

About students: first name, grade, and club assignment only. We do not collect a student's email, photo, address, or any other personal information. A student's login is created and controlled by the parent.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Our students are under 13. Accounts are created by a parent or guardian, who provides verifiable consent at signup. Student-facing pages contain no advertising, no third-party trackers, and no public profiles. Parents may review, export, or request deletion of their family's information at any time from the Family account page.

Communication

There is no private messaging between adults and children in the portal and no student-to-student messaging. Students may send one-way structured feedback to their book club leader; parents can always see it and directors can audit it.

Who can see what

Book club leaders see student first names, grade, and feedback for their own club only. Mailing addresses and parent contact details are visible only to SPARKS directors and the person placing orders, and only when preparing a shipment. Every view of shipping details is logged.

Service providers

Our portal runs on Supabase (database and login), Netlify (website hosting), Resend (email notifications), and the United States Postal Service address-verification service. Mailing addresses are shared with our book vendor (e.g., Amazon Business) solely to ship materials.

Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We keep family records while a student is enrolled and delete them on request or within one year of withdrawal.
