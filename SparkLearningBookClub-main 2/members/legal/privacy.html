Privacy Policy

SPARKS Book Club, Inc. · Version 2026-10 · Effective when published on sparkslearning.org · Questions: becca@sparkslearning.org

SPARKS Book Club, Inc. ("SPARKS", "we", "us") runs a free virtual book club for elementary school students. This policy explains what information our member portal collects, why, who can see it, and the choices parents and guardians have. We wrote it to be read by busy parents, so it is short and plain. It applies to the member portal at sparkslearning.org/members and to the emails the portal sends.

1. Children's privacy comes first (COPPA)

Our students are under 13. We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by designing the portal so that children give us as little information as possible:

  • Only a parent or guardian can create an account. A child cannot sign up on their own.
  • A student's record holds only three things: first name, grade, and which club they are in. We never ask a child for an email address, a photo, a home address, a phone number, or a birthday.
  • The parent creates and controls the student login (a username and a secret word). The parent can reset it, see everything the student sends, or remove it at any time.
  • Student pages contain no advertising, no third-party tracking, and no public profiles.
  • There is no chat. The portal has no messaging between adults and children and no messaging between children. The only thing a student can send is a short, structured feedback form to their book club leader (a star rating and two short answers). The parent can always read it and SPARKS directors can review it.

By creating a family account and accepting this policy, the parent or guardian gives verifiable consent to this collection. You may withdraw consent at any time by withdrawing your student or requesting deletion (section 7).

2. What we collect from parents and guardians

  • Account details: your name, email address, and password (stored only in protected, hashed form).
  • Contact details: your phone number and an optional emergency contact name and phone.
  • Mailing address: used only to ship your student's monthly book and activity kit. We check the address against the United States Postal Service so packages arrive.
  • Agreements: which version of this policy, the Terms of Service, and the Program Consent you accepted, and when.
  • Portal activity: a log of important actions in the portal (for example "address changed" or "student withdrawn") with the date and the account that did it. The log stores identifiers, never the address or contact details themselves.

We do not collect payment information in the portal, and we do not buy or sell personal information.

3. How we use it

  • To mail books and materials to your student before each session.
  • To show you and your student the current book, the session schedule, and a calendar feed you can subscribe to.
  • To let your student share feedback with their leader after a session.
  • To send you program emails: account confirmation, password reset, and notices about your student's sessions. We do not send marketing email from the portal.
  • To run the program: approving book orders, tracking shipments, and keeping simple spending records for our bookkeeping.

4. Who can see what

  • You see everything about your own family and nothing about other families.
  • Your student sees their own book, schedule, and feedback. They cannot see other students, any addresses, or any adult's contact details.
  • Book club leaders (volunteers) see the first names, grades, and feedback of students in their own club only. Leaders cannot see mailing addresses, parent names, emails, or phone numbers.
  • SPARKS directors can see all records so they can run the program. Mailing addresses and parent contact details appear only when a director prepares a shipment, and every time that screen is opened it is recorded in the log.

5. Service providers we rely on

The portal runs on a small number of reputable services. Each one only receives what it needs to do its job, and none of them may use the information for their own purposes:

  • Supabase hosts the portal's database and handles sign-in. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Netlify hosts the website pages.
  • Resend delivers our program emails.
  • United States Postal Service address verification confirms mailing addresses.
  • Our book vendor (currently Amazon Business, possibly a bookstore in the future) receives your name and mailing address solely to ship the monthly package. Their own privacy policy applies to the shipment.
  • Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook receive session dates only if you choose to add the calendar feed. The feed contains session titles, times, and book titles, never addresses.

If we add a service that touches student information, we will update this policy and ask you to accept the new version.

6. Security

Access to every record is controlled by role, so each person sees only what section 4 describes. Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are never stored in readable form. Mailing addresses are never placed in web addresses or logs. Directors use individual accounts, and their actions are logged.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Review and update: everything we hold about your family is on your Family account page, where you can change it.
  • Download: the "Your Data" tab gives you a complete copy of your family's information in a standard file.
  • Withdraw a student: one click on the Students tab. The student stops receiving packages and their login no longer shows a club.
  • Delete: use "Request account deletion" on the Your Data tab or email us. We delete the family's records, including the student logins, within 30 days. We may keep anonymous order and spending totals for bookkeeping.
  • Withdraw consent: you may do so at any time by the steps above.

8. Retention

We keep family records while a student is enrolled and for up to one year after withdrawal, in case the student rejoins. After that, or sooner on request, records are deleted.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, you will be asked to read and accept the new version the next time you sign in. Previous versions and the dates you accepted them remain on record.

10. Contact
